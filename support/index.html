Common questions

Why did a launch time change?

Launch schedules can move because of weather, technical checks, range availability, or provider updates. Mission Control refreshes public sources regularly, but final timing is always subject to change.

Why is live data temporarily unavailable?

Some features depend on public third-party services. If a provider is delayed, rate-limited, or offline, Mission Control shows a clearly labeled unavailable or last-updated state until the service recovers.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

  • Confirm notifications are enabled for Mission Control in iPhone Settings.
  • Confirm the relevant alert is enabled inside the app.
  • Check whether Focus or Do Not Disturb is suppressing notifications.

Why is a widget not updating?

  • Open Mission Control once to refresh recent data.
  • Confirm the device has network access.
  • Remove and re-add the widget if the issue persists.

Do I need an account?

No. Mission Control does not require account creation or sign-in.

What to include in a support request

  • Device model and iOS version
  • Mission Control version
  • A short description of the issue
  • The date and approximate time it occurred
  • Screenshots, if helpful and free of personal information
